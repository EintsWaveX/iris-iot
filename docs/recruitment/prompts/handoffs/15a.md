### 2. Slotku and the phone rubric sheet (r4-slotku, r4-lembar)
- Slotku lists only the examiner's own slots: the room and time, the other examiner, the candidates in order with expected time and QR attendance, sheets done, the slot's QR screen (S2's wall), and one primary action, Nilai <name>. A later slot's sheets open when it starts.
- The sheet is the kit Drawer, full width at 390 with the action in its sticky foot; on a desktop the wide drawer, rubric left, context right.
- **Slot strip** on top: one pill per candidate (time, first name, Terkirim, Tidak Hadir, Sekarang, Belum); tap to switch; the body slides 10 px that way (180 ms, power3.out). On short screens the pills collapse to one line with a coloured bottom edge.
- **Candidate header:** number and expected time, full name, NIM and class, QR badge, and Mulai Wawancara, a clock against 11 minutes that turns gold past it and never blocks.
- **Rows:** label, weight, the question to ask, the control. A 1 to 5 row is a segmented radio of five targets of at least 48 px (words under the numbers from 600 px, and the chosen word in the row header always). A longer scale is a big stepper; an empty one starts at the middle on the first tap.
- **Send:** Kirim Lembar is never dead. Until complete it reads "Kirim Lembar · N lagi" and pressing it scrolls to the first empty row and rings it. Complete: the score out of 100 shows in the foot. Sent: the check draws in (420 ms), how long it stays editable (until Cutting Wawancara starts), and **the next candidate in my slot** with time and QR state and one button. A sent sheet has Simpan Perubahan and Tarik Lembar.
- Notes for the Pleno with three quick phrases; Panitia only.
- **Tidak Hadir** asks first, offers Susulan Wawancara (Jumat, 4 Desember 2026) and moves on. **No signal:** the draft stays on the phone and Kirim queues the sheet.
- The other examiner's values stay hidden until mine is sent (S3's hidden peer sheets). The Tes Tulis score is not on the sheet. The one-line motivation from Daftar is.

### 3. Penilaian (r4-nilai)
- Tally: sheets in of expected, candidates 20 or more points apart, waiting for a sheet, absent and offered Susulan.
- Per candidate: a chip per examiner sheet (code and score; mine in the accent), the average to one decimal, the spread as a strip on the real 0 to 100 scale (a dot per examiner, the range shaded, the average as a tick), the gap in points (amber from 12, red from 20), and the Tes Tulis total muted, for context. Sort by average, gap or slot; filter wide gaps, missing sheets, absent. Phone: cards with the same strip.
- Rincian: rubric rows against examiners with the gap per row, the most disputed row marked, and the notes.
- **Rata-Rata per Penguji:** each examiner's average against the average of every sheet, as a bar either side of the line, with their sheet count on hover, so a split panel can be read fairly.

## Contracts (for 15b)
- **Tables:** `rec_slot_examiner` already exists (0051: `slot_id`, `asisten_nim`, `role` enum `["pengawas"]`, PK on all three). Wawancara examiners need a role value (`penguji`) or a reuse decision; next free migration is **0055** (check `apps/web/drizzle/` first). `rec_rubric` (`stage`, `kind`, `rows`) and `rec_rubric_score` (`subject_id`, `grader_nim`, `values`, `note`) exist from 0052; for Wawancara `subject_id` is the candidate id (Live Coding uses the seat id), and the rubric is `stage: "wawancara"`.
- **RubricRow** gains an optional `min` (default 0): interview rows read 1 to 5. Sheet score = round(100 × Σ weight × (v − min) / (max − min) ÷ Σ weight). A row whose `max − min` is 4 or less renders as segments, otherwise as the stepper.
- **Candidate score** = mean of their sheet scores; **spread** = max − min of sheet scores; wide gap from 20 (sample).
- **Conflict rules:** examiner clash = one `asisten_nim` in two slots whose `[starts_at, ends_at)` overlap; candidate clash = two `rec_slot_assignment` rows of one candidate (any stage) whose slots overlap; over capacity = assignments > `capacity`; short = examiners < the per-slot minimum. Flags only, never a block.
- **Rights (proposed):** examiners see only their own slots and candidates; candidates never see examiners; Penilaian in full for the Ketua, Akademik and Admin; any other examiner sees their own sheets; a peer's values stay hidden from an examiner until their own sheet for that candidate is sent. Sheets open at slot start and lock when Cutting Wawancara starts.
- **Live:** examiner lane changes go out on `recplotting` like S2's moves.
- **Nav:** with a ninth panel tab (Wawancara) RecNav keeps 7 and folds the rest into Lainnya from 1100 to 1439 px (English overflowed at 1280 otherwise). The app's `RecNav.tsx` needs the same rule.
- Keyframes `rec4-*` in the mockup map to `rec-s4-*` in the app; classes `r4`.
