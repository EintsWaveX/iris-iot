- **The way in:** `TestDoor` on the candidate's page, a card that appears when the candidate's room is open (the `recroom` topic, no reload, no timer) and goes away at Finish.

- **The seven renderers** (`exam/render/`, shared with 09's preview): single, checkbox, click-lines, pick-the-fix (kit Dropdown with highlighted lines inside), order-lines (`@dnd-kit` sortable, keyboard Space/arrows/Space and pointer), match-pairs (kit Dropdown, a held choice moves and the loser flashes), FITB (the IRIS blank, several to a line, in code or in the prompt). - **The runner** (`Runner.tsx`): top bar, navigator (rail from 820 px, strip on a phone), one question at a time with the 10 px slide, Tandai, Kumpulkan dialog with what is left, the Referensi drawer, the save chip, the relay chip. - **Time** is the server's: `seatPhase()` (`lib/recruitment/seat-state.ts`) builds a room state out of what the relay and `GET test` hand a seat and asks the same `room.ts` functions the API asks. The page redraws once a second and arms one timer (`pada`) for the seat's own end, where the last autosave leaves.
- **Autosave** (`AnswerSaver`): per change, 400 ms debounce, one value per question, rev from the server, unsent answers in `localStorage` and retried at 2, 5, 10, 15 s; a refused answer (`waktu_habis`, `sudah_kumpul`) is dropped and the chip says so.
- **Soft lockdown** (`lockdown/useLockdown.ts`, rules pure in `seat-lockdown.ts`): fullscreen left (the cover shows at once), tab hidden, focus lost (a click into the reference frame is not a loss), paste (blocked), devtools keys and a devtools-sized window; copy, cut, context menu, print and select-all outside a field are blocked and not counted. Strikes merge within 1.5 s, are sent when they open and when they close (`keepalive`), queue offline, and a reloaded page ends what its earlier life left open (`resume`).
- **The API** (`api/_lib/recruitment-seat.ts`, 7 actions): `test`, `test-ticket`, `test-checkin`, `test-answer`, `test-strike`, `test-submit`, `test-hotspot`. Every action takes the seat from the oprec session (NIM to candidate to seat), never from the request.
- **The hotspot field** (`HotspotCard.tsx`, 9.6a): `{ ssid, pass }` in, `{ ssid }` out; the password is sealed by 11's `simpanHotspotKursi` and never comes back; `{ clear: true }` hands the boards back to the pengawas hotspots.
