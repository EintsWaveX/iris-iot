## Contracts
- The modules change only additively; a rename, a removal or a new meaning goes to the reviewer by SendMessage first (EXAM-ENGINE 10.4).
- `act()` returns `{ ok, state, effects }` or `{ ok: false, kode }`. Effects: `spin-rest`, `lc-close`, `reroll`. Control actions need `seq` equal to the stored one (`versi_lama` otherwise); Master actions (`claim`, `ask`, `give`, `decline`, `release`) do not, and the API's `WHERE seq = $seen` update guards them.
- `ActCtx` is what the API supplies: `now`, `nim`, `canBeMaster(nim)`, `masterStale`, `isToday`, `rehearsal`, `seatsIn`, `poolOk(part)`, `config`.
- `scorePart` takes real seats only (rehearsal seats never go in) and one part at a time. Missing credits are unanswered (0, still shown).
- Ids in fixtures may be 1 to 12 characters of `a` to `z` and `0` to `9`; `newId()` always makes 6.
