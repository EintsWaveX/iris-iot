### Design calls 06 follows once signed off
- **Panel tabs:** Cutting (every Panitia member with the propose right) and Plotting (Ketua, Akademik, Admin) join between Kandidat and Panitia. On a phone the bar shows four and puts the rest in a Lainnya sheet.
- **Berkas review:** the drawer widens to about 1180 px for Admin and Ketua: PdfViewer left, checks right (Sesuai or Perbaiki per file, kit chip buttons), Nilai Berkas kept and badged "Menunggu keputusan" (dash until set, ignored by Cutting), V1 and V2 when a fix came back, a waiting queue with arrows and Setujui moving to the next one.
- **Cutting:** stage line tabs (a tab appears when its Cutting starts); a tally card (Lolos, Gagal, not yet, one stacked bar, the deck target) with the Ketua's lock card beside it; table with rank, FM/TT attendance pills, four parts, total of 4000, Lolos/Gagal chips, the proposer's code, a note popover. Overwriting someone's proposal says whose in the toast. Usulan Ambang: Top N or a minimum score on a page-drawn slider over a chart drawn to the same scale, live counts against 60 to 80, "no-shows fail" on, "replace existing" off, a dashed line in the table where the cut falls. Berkas stage: one bulk action from the document status. Lock is a 1.6 s hold, off while anyone lacks a proposal. Locked: card with the reveal countdown, Buka Kunci (asks first) only before the reveal. After the reveal: how many opened, an Amplop column.
- **Envelope:** sealed hero card on the candidate page plus a row in the inbox; the page never spoils a sealed result (journey, badge and Pilih Jadwal stay as they were until it's opened). Opening happens on a full-frame stage over the page. Result copy: Lolos names the next event and offers Pilih Jadwal; Gagal lists what they got through, no scores; Penerimaan draws the eight stars.
- **Pilih Jadwal:** picker as a large dialog with the three evenings side by side (kit `.slot` cards, seat pips, rolling numbers), then a confirm step; a lost race returns to the list with a note. After picking, a ticket card; Ajukan Perubahan dialog (target with room, 3 quick reasons, 10 characters minimum); pending, approved and declined notes on the card; off after H-1 with who to see. Berikutnya shows the candidate's own evening once picked.
- **Plotting:** Slot (window card from the Calendar event with Perpanjang Sehari and Tutup Sekarang, per evening rows with room, kit TimeField, Stepper never below taken), Permintaan (approve, or Setujui Melebihi Kapasitas when the target is full; decline with an optional note), Papan (useDnd board, Belum memilih column, Auto and Panitia marks, drop on a full room asks first). Auto Isi: emptiest room first, NIM order, deterministic, one Urungkan for the run.
- **QR:** `/recruitment/qr` without navbar: QR with a 20 s drain ring, event, rolling count, 78 dots, no names on the wall, a 6-character code for phones that can't scan (`/hadir`, valid for its period plus one). Scan results: hadir, sudah, bukan jadwalmu (with their own slot), QR berganti (type the code). Kehadiran drawer from Ringkasan's Hari Ini card while an attended event runs: Tandai Hadir with three reasons; manual marks can be undone, QR ones can't.
- **Calendar seed:** three `pick` events (Pilih Jadwal Tes Tulis 11 to 14 Nov, Wawancara 27 to 29 Nov, Mengajar 20 to 21 Dec, each from 12:00) with their own kind colour (#f472b6), as PRD section 3 lists them.

### Particles decision
**canvas-confetti 1.9.3** for particles, **GSAP** (already in the app) for every timeline. One canvas beats 150 to 300 DOM nodes for the Penerimaan bursts on a phone; it gives star shapes from a path, a worker option (use it in production; it's off in the mockup) and `disableForReducedMotion`. GSAP-only would mean writing gravity, drift and decay ourselves. New dependency for 06: `canvas-confetti` (about 4 KB gzip), lazy-loaded with the reveal island.

## Motion specs (implement exactly)
Easing names are GSAP's. "full" = Normal and HD, "ldm" = Low Detail (the site default), "reduce" = prefers-reduced-motion or Kurangi Gerak.

**Envelope, sealed**
- Idle float: y 0 to -6 px, 1.6 s each way, `sine.inOut`, yoyo, forever. full only.
- Edge: the kit's `--sweep` ring at 1.5 px with the `sweep` keyframes (9 s linear), the same as the Home banner. ldm and reduce: still.
- Inbox mini envelope while sealed: the kit's rainbow edge (`edgespin`, 6 s linear). reduce: still.

**Envelope, opening (input)**
- Drag: flap rotateX 0 to 150 deg over 170 px of upward drag (220 px for Penerimaan); the seal rides the flap tip (translateY = flapHeight × (cos θ − 1)). Release opens past 50 percent or above 0.6 px/ms upward; otherwise back to 0 in 220 ms `power3.out`.
- Hold (pointer anywhere on the envelope, or Space/Enter on the seal): a ring around the seal fills in 1100 ms linear; past 62 percent a shiver (rotate ±0.6 deg, 90 ms alternate) in full only; release drains the ring in 200 ms `power3.out`. reduce keeps the ring (feedback, not motion).

**Lolos (from the moment it opens)**
1. 0 ms: the seal splits; each half x ±12 px, y 6 px, rotate ±20 deg, fade, 260 ms `power2.out`; the mark scales to 0.6 and fades (200 ms); 14 gold sparks (canvas, spread 360, velocity 9, 70 ticks; none in ldm). The chime starts (4 notes, 90 ms apart).
2. 80 ms: flap rotateX to 180 deg, 520 ms `power3.inOut`; z-index drops behind the letter at 340 ms.
3. 450 ms: letter yPercent −62, 640 ms `power4.out`.
4. 1000 ms: FLIP: the result card jumps to the risen letter's rect (x, y, scale), the letter hides, the card goes to identity in 560 ms `power3.out`; the envelope y +46 px and fades, 420 ms `power3.out`.
5. 1000 ms: bloom (conic kosmik gradient, blur 48 px) scale 0.6 to 1.25, opacity 0 to 0.6 in 700 ms `power2.out`, then to scale 1.4, opacity 0.22 in 1100 ms `sine.out`. full only.
6. 1050 ms: confetti from both bottom corners (80 each, angle 62/118, spread 62, velocity 56, gravity 0.9, 240 ticks, kosmik colours, stars + circles + squares); 260 ms later a centre burst (60, spread 120, velocity 34; full only). ldm: 24 per corner, no centre burst.
7. 1180 ms: copy items y 10 to 0 and fade in, 360 ms `power3.out`, 60 ms stagger.

**Gagal (no sound)**
1. 0 ms: seal rotates −24 deg and fades, 420 ms `sine.out`.
2. 100 ms: flap to 180 deg, 700 ms `sine.inOut`; z-index drop at 450 ms.
3. 300 ms: sky dim (`--r2-dim` 0 to 1, overlay rgba(8,14,32,0.5)), 900 ms `sine.inOut`.
4. 820 ms: letter yPercent −42, 800 ms `sine.out`.
5. 1500 ms: FLIP as above, 700 ms `sine.out`; envelope sinks as above.
6. 1700 ms: soft rain, 28 drops (1.5 px × 9 percent tall, opacity 0.16 to 0.36), each 1.6 to 2.4 s linear with up to 1.4 s random delay, two passes, then removed. full only.
7. 2000 ms: copy, 420 ms `sine.out`, 90 ms stagger.
8. 2220 ms: exhale, scaleY 0.975 scaleX 1.012 rotate −1 deg in 500 ms `sine.inOut`, then scale 1, rotate −0.6 deg in 700 ms `sine.out`.

**Penerimaan:** Lolos with a bigger envelope (gold IRIS seal), the chime with 5 notes, then at 1100 ms the stage line draws (strokeDashoffset 100 to 0, 900 ms `power2.inOut`) and the 8 stars pop (scale 0.4 to 1, 320 ms `back.out(2)`, 110 ms stagger); after the two corner bursts and the centre burst, four firework bursts (46 each, spread 360, velocity 26, gravity 0.7) 520 ms apart from 700 ms. ldm: as ldm Lolos plus the stars.

**reduce (all results):** no movement. Envelope fades out and the card fades in (opacity, 280 ms ease). Lolos and Penerimaan get 14 still stars printed in a ring around the card (fade in 280 ms). Gagal: no stars and no rain.

**Replay:** reset all GSAP props (`context.revert()`), clear the canvas, back to sealed.

**Elsewhere**
- Stage enters: opacity 0 to 1, 260 ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1).
- Seat numbers: the old number leaves, the new slides in from −70 percent (down) or +70 percent (up), 180 ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1). reduce: swap.
- Approved ticket: one flash from ok 26 percent to the surface, 1400 ms ease-out. reduce: none.
- Berkas queue: body opacity 0 to 1 with x ±10 px in the direction moved, 180 ms `power3.out`.
- Cutting lock: lock card fades y +12 px in 200 ms `power3.out`, then the locked card drops in (y −8 px to 0, opacity, 300 ms). Tally bar segments `flex-grow` 280 ms. Threshold cut line `left` 120 ms.
- Hold buttons: fill scaleX 0 to 1 over the hold (1600 ms linear for the lock), release 200 ms.
- Auto Isi: FLIP every moved chip from its old rect, 420 ms `power3.out`, 20 ms stagger (cap 20). reduce: none.
- New change request arrives: card y −8 px to 0 and fades in, 260 ms. reduce: none.
- QR wall: new code opacity 0 to 1 from scale 0.98, 240 ms `power3.out`; ring drains 20 s linear; a new arrival's dot pops from scale 0.4 and white, 600 ms (not in ldm or reduce).
- Scan check: circle and tick draw (strokeDashoffset 100 to 0), 420 ms `power3.out`.
- Every keyframe name starts with `rec-s2-`.
