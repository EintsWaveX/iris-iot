## Ruling recorded here
**Hotspots can be each candidate's own phone (user, 2026-10-24; EXAM-ENGINE 9.6a, WAVE3.md).** A candidate types their own phone's SSID and password on the Live Coding seat page. It becomes a hotspot row owned by that seat, with `cap` equal to the seat's Wi-Fi boards, and those boards go there first. Pengawas hotspots stay as the fallback, and a seat with no phone is never blocked. The mockup shows it in three places: the seat page card (10 owns it), the room setup rows and the console grouping (11), and Kompilasi Ulang after a change (12). The design calls are below under each owner.

## Motion specs (implement exactly)
Easing names are GSAP's; `ease-out` means cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1). "full" = Normal and HD, "ldm" = Low Detail (the site default), "reduce" = prefers-reduced-motion or Kurangi Gerak. Every keyframe name starts with `rec3-` in the mockup and `rec-s3-` in the app (agreed with 06).

**Topic spin** (cell height 76 px; the column's y puts cell i in the middle at −76 × (i − 1))
1. Press: the column moves y +10 px in 140 ms `power2.out` (the pull of a lever). The button reads Memutar and is off.
2. Steady run: 14 cells at 55 ms a cell, `none` (linear), while lc-spin is in flight. If the answer is late, the run repeats until it arrives. Decoy titles are blurred 3 px (CSS) and the column blurs 0 to 2.4 px over the first 200 ms.
3. Decelerate onto the answer's cell minus 16 percent of a cell in 2.1 s `power3.out`; the column blur clears over the last 900 ms (`power2.out`).
4. Settle onto the cell in 360 ms `back.out(2.2)`.
5. Land: the window's ring goes from opacity 0.35 to 1 (240 ms) and runs the kit sweep (`sweep`, 6 s linear); the landed title un-blurs from 4 px and opacity 0.4 (240 ms ease-out); its glyph tile turns recruitment gold. The protocol reel's Putar wakes.
- ldm: no blur anywhere, 45 ms a cell, deceleration 1.2 s, the ring stands still.
- reduce: no movement; the column jumps to the answer, the title fades in (280 ms ease), the ring is still.

**Protocol spin**: the same shape with 8 steady cells and a 1.5 s deceleration (ldm 0.9 s).

**Automatic and re-roll**: the same timelines without a press: the topic reel starts 500 ms after the page gets the result, the protocol 1100 ms after the topic lands. Labels Otomatis or Diundi ulang oleh <code>.

**Part transitions**
- Start (lobby to part): the lobby card fades out with scale 0.98 (180 ms ease-out); the top bar and question card rise 8 px and fade in (220 ms ease-out, the card 60 ms after the bar); navigator cells fade in with a 20 ms stagger (cap 15). The timer number never animates.
- Between questions: the card slides 10 px from the side it came from, 180 ms ease-out.
- Time up: at the seat's own deadline the content goes blur 0 to 6 px and opacity to 0.5 in 300 ms ease-out (inert at once); the Waktu habis card scales in from 0.96 with a fade, 240 ms ease-out, 200 ms later; after 1.2 s the page crossfades to the lobby (200 ms). ldm: no blur, opacity 0.18. reduce: opacity fades only.
- Lobby: the finished part's segment fills from the left (scaleX 0 to 1, 400 ms ease-out); the waiting dot breathes (opacity 1 to 0.4, 1.6 s ease-in-out, forever; still at reduce).
- Check-in rows: each check's icon scales in from 0.96 (220 ms ease-out) as it passes; the spinner turns 700 ms a turn (2.4 s at reduce).

**Fullscreen cover**: blur and inert apply instantly (integrity first); the backdrop fades in 120 ms ease-out; the card rises 12 px and fades in 200 ms ease-out with a 40 ms delay; the button presses to scale 0.97. Return: the cover is removed at once. reduce: opacity only (160 ms).

**Compile and Flash**
- Queue: chips re-render as the `antrean` message moves; the candidate's position number rolls like S2's seat numbers (old out, new in from ±70 percent, 180 ms ease-out; reduce: swap).
- Compiling: a 36 percent wide sweep stripe slides across the track, 1.2 s linear, forever. ldm: a full bar blinking opacity (1.2 s ease-in-out). reduce: a still half-opacity bar.
- Done: the bar is full and green. Failed: full and red; the log rises 8 px and fades in (200 ms ease-out); an error link scrolls the editor smoothly to the line and flashes its background danger to danger-soft (1.2 s ease-out). reduce: instant scroll, no flash.
- Flash: a determinate bar (scaleX, 120 ms linear per update); retry turns it warn and resets.

**Elsewhere**: buttons, tiles and option cards press to scale 0.97 to 0.985 (140 ms ease-out); match rows that lose their pair flash gold (1.2 s ease-out); handover cards rise 8 px (260 ms); the away dot on a tile blinks (1.1 s; still at reduce).
