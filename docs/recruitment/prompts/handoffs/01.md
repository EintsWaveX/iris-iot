## Design calls (for 03 to follow once signed off)
- **Banner:** one card tall, under the practicum top. Its edge is the kit's `--sweep` gradient with the `sweep` keyframes as a 1.5 px ring; the card stays dark glass with a soft gold wash. Recruitment's hue is the Profil Lab Rekruitasi gold (`--rec`). At most two buttons. On a phone the buttons fill equally.
- **Candidate page:**
  - Layout: two columns from 1100 px (main plus a 360 px sticky side). On a phone, Berikutnya comes first.
  - Journey: a constellation of eight stars. The done part of the path draws in once. The current star is gold with a slow halo (static at LDM or Kurangi). Each star opens a popover with that stage's public dates. On a phone it becomes a vertical rail.
- **Calendar:**
  - The KalenderTp grid, extended with lanes, labels that span the bar within the week, internal events hatched and dashed (Panitia only), and kind colours.
  - Editing for Ketua and Acara: drag to move, drag the round ends to resize, click to edit. While dragging, the legend row shows the new dates live. Every change gives a toast with Urungkan. A started stage asks first (matches 02's 409 `perlu_konfirmasi`).
- **Daftar:** a dialog with a 3-step numbered stepper. Name, NIM and class are read only from the account. The motivation is one sentence of up to 140 characters. Three PDF slots, each with Unduh template and its own error. Consent is a Checkbox naming the 6-month deletion. The success state is small and quiet; the big party is saved for the envelopes.
- **Panel:**
  - Only the S1 tabs are shown. Ringkasan has three equal-height cards (funnel, today and next deadline, my division's tasks).
  - Kandidat is a table from 820 px and a list below that. Its wide drawer holds details, documents (Admin and Ketua only), the fix note and Panitia notes. Approve and Ask for a Fix (with quick reasons) sit in the drawer foot.
  - Entry points: the banner, and Lab menu › Rekrutmen while an edition is live.
- **Panitia board:** the kit's useDnd (like Konsol's group moves). Six columns. A star marks the one Koordinator per division. The Ketua seat is locked to Pencipta/Admin. Long names wrap to 2 lines.
