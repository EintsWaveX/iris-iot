# Fact Sheet — Module 1: Bluetooth Connectivity

## Plain-language explainer
Bluetooth is a short-range wireless technology that links two devices directly, with no central server (peer-to-peer). This module covers two paths: **Bluetooth Classic** (Master/Slave, sending data over a "serial" link as if wired) and **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)** (Central/Peripheral, sending data through the more power-efficient GATT structure) — BLE is new material added this year.

## Objectives (from the official module)
1. Learn Bluetooth technology.
2. Understand device-to-device communication using Bluetooth.
3. Implement Bluetooth for an IoT system.

## Hardware used
ESP32, Arduino IDE/PlatformIO, sensor & actuator (optional).

## Shareable facts
- The name "Bluetooth" comes from Viking King Harald "Bluetooth" Gormsson, who united Denmark and Norway in the 10th century — chosen as a symbol of "uniting" devices under one wireless standard. (Official Bluetooth SIG source below.)
- Bluetooth was first developed by a team of Ericsson engineers in the 1990s, led by Jaap Haartsen.
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) was first introduced with Bluetooth 4.0 (2010) — built specifically so small-battery devices can run for a long time, making it a natural fit for IoT sensors.
- Bluetooth 6.0 (2024) introduced Channel Sounding, enabling device-to-device positioning down to centimeter-level accuracy.
