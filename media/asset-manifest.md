# Asset Manifest

All paths below are relative to the project root (`IRIS ASO/`) and were confirmed to exist in the repository. Nothing here is a screenshot cropped out of the PDF — these are the original clean source images the PDF's own figures were built from, so they're higher quality than anything you'd extract from `MODUL REG.pdf` itself.

Format note: most of these are portrait/landscape diagrams — for Instagram square/portrait feed posts, place the diagram on a branded background card (navy `#0d2444` background, `#40b0ff` accent, IRIS logo corner-mark) rather than posting the bare PNG edge-to-edge.
